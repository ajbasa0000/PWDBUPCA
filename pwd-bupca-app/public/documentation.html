E Executive Overview & Organization Mandate

The Barangay UP Campus Persons with Disability Association (PWD BUPCA Inc.) is a grassroots community initiative uniting artisans, seamstresses, and residents with determination. This integrated digital ecosystem digitizes all operational workflows:

1. Public Intake & Inclusion

5-step guided registration with Filipino/English toggle, Natural Voice TTS audio playback, reference code tracking, and RA 10173 Data Privacy compliance.

2. Physical Asset & MR Registry

Complete inventory tracking for 19 authentic sewing and edging machines, real custodian allocation, turnover records, and official printable Memorandum Receipts.

3. Skills & Field Mobility

Community training demand analysis, cohort machine pairing, official 2-sided PVC credential cards, and door-to-door Pook Encoder offline tablet mode.

1 Physical Machinery & Memorandum Receipt (MR) Module

Every machine owned or managed by PWD BUPCA is serialized, tagged, and tied to an accountable artisan custodian under an enforceable Memorandum Receipt slip.

Machine Categories in Active Service

  • Old Sewing Machines (Lola Makina): China lockstitch machines for traditional heavy denim patchwork and bag panels (e.g., Custodian: Rosa Zalun).
  • Industrial High-Speed Machines: Juki L-1A direct-drive lockstitch units for fast volume production (e.g., Custodian: Joyzel San Valentin).
  • Industrial Edging Machines: Siruba & Juki M1-424NS-F 4-thread differential overlock machines for clean seams and durable tote edges.
  • Singer Portable Sewing Machines: Flexible portables for household solo parents and artisans working from home.

Custodianship & Turnover Protocol

The Memorandum Receipt (MR) serves as a binding legal instrument between PWD BUPCA and the artisan:

Accountability Clause: The artisan custodian agrees to exercise proper care, perform periodic oiling, notify leadership of mechanical faults, and return the asset upon cessation of membership.

2 Public Registration & Administrative Review Hub

The public registration system (`/membership`) provides an accessible, dignified entry point into the association for community residents, coupled with an admin screening workflow (`/admin`).

1. Multilingual & TTS

Authentic Tagalog/English toggle with Filipino speech synthesis for every step, ensuring visually-impaired residents can listen to question instructions.

2. Intersectoral Inclusion

Captures 13 official disability classifications (RA 7277), PDAO ID status, and overlapping sectors (Solo Parents, Women Survivors, OSY, Seniors).

3. Admin Screening Hub

Staff screening queue (`Pending`, `Approved`, `Needs Info`), applicant dossier view, one-click CSV export, and membership ID generation.

Applicant Reference & Tracking Mechanism

Upon submitting, applicants receive an auto-generated tracking code (e.g., BUPCA-APP-2026-0042). They can return to `/membership` at any time to verify application progress and, upon approval, immediately view their official Digital Member ID Card.

4 Digital Membership Card & Printable Credential Generator

Approved members are issued official CR-80 wallet-sized identification credentials complying with national Magna Carta standards (RA 7277) and Quezon City PDAO verification rules.

Official Front Design Specifications

  • Header: Official PWD BUPCA logo, association seal, and verified member badge.
  • Member Particulars: Legal Full Name, Assigned Membership ID (BUPCA-MEM-xxxx), QC PDAO ID.
  • Disability Classification: Category and apparent/non-apparent visibility indicator.
  • Residency: Street and Pook sector in Barangay UP Campus.

Official Back Design Specifications

  • Emergency Data: Medical hotlines, guardian contact details, and blood type.
  • Intersectoral Sector: Solo Parent, Senior, OSY, or Women's Sector.
  • Signatures: Member signature line and President Marilyn Morales authorization signature.
  • Security QR Code: Dynamic code ready for field verification.

Batch Credential Print Modes

Administrators can print ID cards on standard A4 or Letter cardstock / PVC sheet printer via the Batch ID Card Credential Printer:
• Front Only: Single-pass front face printing.
• Back Only: Single-pass reverse side printing.
• Both Sides (2-Pass): Paired front and back layout with Pook filtering (e.g. print all Pook Amorsolo or Pook Dagohoy cards in one click).

5 Field Worker & Pook Encoder Tablet Mode (PWA-Ready)

Designed to solve the "last-mile" problem where bedridden, low-vision, or under-documented residents cannot commute to the barangay hall or access the web.

Operational Duties of Pook Encoders

  • Door-to-Door Census: Conducting guided household interviews in Pook Amorsolo, Dagohoy, Palaris, Area 1, Area 2, etc.
  • Audio-Assisted Questioning: Triggering Filipino voice prompts for residents who cannot read or have visual impairments.
  • PDAO Gap Identification: Flagging residents without QC PDAO IDs so association officers can facilitate registration.
  • Equipment Needs Assessment: Checking if the resident has a sewing machine at home or needs a BUPCA machine loan.

Offline Autosave & Batch Synchronization

The tablet console runs entirely client-side with full offline resiliency:

  • Offline Storage: Automatically stores field interviews in local browser memory when internet connection is lost.
  • Status Sentinel: Real-time indicator displaying Online Ready or Offline Storage Active.
  • One-Click Batch Sync: When back in range of community Wi-Fi, clicking Batch Sync merges all saved records into the Admin review queue.

6 Technical Architecture, Stack & Regulatory Framework

System engineering details and statutory governance guaranteeing long-term maintainability and resident privacy.

Next.js 16 (App Router)

Turbopack compilation, React 19 server/client components, zero external heavy database lock-in, client-side localStorage caching.

Accessibility Architecture

WCAG 2.1 AAA high-contrast modes (Black/Yellow, Dark), Dyslexic typography toggle, text magnification, and Web Speech API synthesis.

RA 10173 Data Privacy

Strict explicit consent handling, encrypted local caching, role-based access control (Superuser vs. Admin vs. Artisan operator).
