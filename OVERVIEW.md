## 🏛️ Executive Summary

**PWD BUPCA Inc.** (*Persons with Disabilities - Barangay UP Campus Association Inc.*) is an award-winning grassroots social enterprise located within the **University of the Philippines Diliman, Quezon City**. Honored as the recipient of the prestigious **UP Gawad Tsanselor 2025 para sa Natatanging Samahang Pangkomunidad**, PWD BUPCA champions the shift in societal paradigm: **from dependency to active leadership and economic independence**.

By uniting **circular textile craftsmanship**, **ergonomically adapted industrial machinery**, and an **accessibility-first digital command center**, the organization provides accredited vocational skills and guaranteed fair living wages to artisans with mobility, visual, hearing, speech, and neurodivergent determinations.

## 🎯 Three Pillars of Impact

### 1. For Sponsors & Institutional Partners (CSR, ESG & Academic Allies)
* **Auditable Social & Environmental ROI:**  
  Every sponsorship directly funds raw material subsidies, machine maintenance, and specialized vocational certifications in partnership with the **UP College of Home Economics (UP CHE)**, **UP College of Fine Arts**, and the **UP Center for Women's and Gender Studies**.
* **Circular ESG Metrics:**  
  Over **2.4 tons** of discarded denim pants, uniform excesses, and post-consumer textile offcuts are diverted from Philippine landfills each year and transformed into durable, retail-grade utility goods.
* **Corporate & B2B Bulk Solutions:**  
  A built-in B2B production pipeline capable of delivering customized convention kits, eco-conference tote bags, laptop cases, and corporate gift packages with complete artisan attribution and impact certificates.

### 2. For the General Public & Conscious Consumers
* **Handcrafted Quality with Authentic Provenance:**  
  Every product sold through the virtual store ([`/store`](/store)) carries the story of the artisan who crafted it, highlighting the machine used, workshop station, and zero-waste fabrication techniques.
* **100% Direct Labor Reinvestment:**  
  Store proceeds directly support member wages, workshop utilities, and community welfare funds.
* **Transparent Frictionless Purchasing:**  
  Modern digital storefront supporting instant GCash, BPI UP Campus bank transfers, and direct on-campus pickup at Pook Dagohoy.

### 3. For Persons with Disabilities (The PWD Community)
* **Barrier-Free Workplace & Dignified Livelihood:**  
  Vocational workstations are ergonomically adapted for wheelchair operators, low-vision craftspeople, and deaf-mute sewers, ensuring equitable access to high-speed industrial machinery.
* **Dual Compensation Security (Piece-Rate + DTR Hourly Allowance):**  
  Members receive both hourly operational allowances for their monitored workshop hours and itemized piece-rates for every finished unit, with 100% free raw materials.
* **Digital Inclusion & Co-Ownership:**  
  Members independently log their daily time records (DTR), request subsidized supplies, and review itemized pay slips through a multi-modal accessible user interface.

## 💻 Technology & Platform Architecture

The platform was built from the ground up to exceed **WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA accessibility standards** while offering a clean, calm, and modern human aesthetic.

### 1. Multi-Modal Accessibility Engine
* **Dynamic Theme Switcher:** Instant toggle between Clean Light and Pure Dark modes with persisted preferences.
* **High-Contrast Assistive Modes:** One-click activation of High-Contrast Yellow/Black (the international gold standard for low-vision and macular degeneration).
* **Font Adaptability:** Instant scaling from 100% up to 150% without layout disruption, coupled with a specialized **OpenDyslexic** typeface toggle.
* **Sensory Chimes & Speech Synthesis:** Web Audio oscillator sound cues accompany check-ins, DTR submissions, and order confirmations, alongside integrated text-to-speech audio guidance.

### 2. Physical & Stationary Operations Tracker
* **Interactive Workshop GIS Map:** Integrates OpenStreetMap and Leaflet to display live status pins for all active machinery across three physical hubs:
  1. *UP CHE Sewing Workshop* (Alonso Hall, UP Diliman)
  2. *Area 2 Community Center* (J.P. Laurel St., Brgy. UP Campus)
  3. *PWD BUPCA Central Headquarters* (Pook Dagohoy)
* **Equipment Status & Relocation History:** Real-time visibility into machine availability (`Online / In-Use`, `Idle`, `Maintenance Tune-Up`) and tracking of machine transfers between workshop nodes.
* **Integrated Station DTR:** Operators clock in directly at machine terminals, automatically logging shift duration, fabric consumed, finished units produced, and piece-rate credits.

### 3. Production & Inventory Operations
* **Subsidized Raw Materials Hub:** Real-time inventory tracking of donated denim, twill liners, heavy thread spools, and brass zippers with automated low-stock warnings and one-click request approvals.
* **Shopping & Fulfillment Hub:** Pipeline for managing customer orders from pending verification through batch production and campus pickup.

### 4. Mathematical Payroll & Auto-Computation Engine
* **Itemized Varied Product Releases:** Accurately accounts for varied product complexities produced within a single pay period:
  $$\text{Hourly Allowance} = \text{Monitored Hours} \times ₱85/\text{hr}$$
  $$\text{Piece-Rate Earnings} = \sum (\text{Units Produced}_i \times \text{Piece Rate}_i)$$
  $$\text{Total Net Take-Home} = \text{Hourly Allowance} + \text{Piece-Rate Earnings} + \text{Bonuses}$$
* **Zero Deduction Guarantee:** All fabrics, notions, and machinery depreciation are covered 100% by PWD BUPCA grants and institutional donors.
* **Printable Pay Stubs:** Generates official itemized statements with UP Gawad Tsanselor insignia for transparent compensation and grant compliance.

### 5. Content Management & Master Database (CMS)
* **No-Code Website CMS:** Empowers community coordinators to publish updates to landing page narratives, testimonials, and verified impact statistics.
* **Curriculum & Programs CRUD:** Full management over vocational trainings, cohort capacities, and academic partnership deliverables.
* **Store Catalog CRUD:** Instant creation and modification of retail products, photos, stock quotas, and labor piece-rate baselines.
* **Master Database Manager:** Centralized administrative control over machinery, member registries, inventory items, and workshop locations.

## 🎓 Accredited Vocational Training Programs

1. **High-Speed Machine Operation & Preventative Maintenance**
   * *Partner:* UP College of Home Economics (UP CHE)
   * *Curriculum:* 24 hours of hands-on certification covering single-needle industrial lockstitch, differential feed 4-thread overlock edging, safety protocols, and ergonomic adaptations.
2. **Eco-Upcycling & Zero-Waste Textile Crafting**
   * *Partner:* UP College of Fine Arts & UP Center for Women's and Gender Studies
   * *Curriculum:* Fabric grading, pattern tessellation for zero-waste offcuts, seam structural engineering, and circular design.
3. **Digital Inventory, DTR & Cooperative Enterprise Accounting**
   * *Partner:* Barangay UP Campus Leadership & Local Enterprise Allies
   * *Curriculum:* Hands-on terminal operation, supply requisition workflows, piece-rate tracking, and financial literacy.
