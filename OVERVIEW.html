PWD BUPCA Inc. — Strategic Platform & Impact Overview

"Transforming Textile Off-Cuts into Inclusive, Dignified Livelihoods & Circular Empowerment"
Persons with Disabilities - Barangay UP Campus Association UP Diliman, Quezon City Social Enterprise & Assistive Technology Official Pitch Deck FY 2026

1. Executive Summary & Mission

PWD BUPCA Inc. is a grassroots community association of Persons with Determination in Barangay UP Campus, Diliman. The organization exists to fundamentally alter societal perceptions of persons with disabilities—transitioning members from dependency into skilled master craftspeople, innovators of ecological circularity, and economic leaders.

Through accredited partnerships with the UP College of Home Economics (UP CHE) and the UP College of Fine Arts, PWD BUPCA combines industrial high-speed sewing, differential overlock edging, and zero-waste fabric tessellation with a purpose-built accessible command center that manages asset tracking, daily time records (DTR), piece-rate labor accounting, and virtual store commerce.

2. Three Stakeholder Impact Pillars

A. For Sponsors, Donors & Institutional Allies (CSR & ESG)

  • Auditable ESG & Social Metrics: Verified circular impact reports tracking metric tons of post-consumer textiles rescued from landfills and converted into commercial goods.
  • Institutional Bulk Manufacturing: High-capacity production pipeline tailored for university conferences, bank merchandise, and corporate gift bags with complete artisan provenance certificates.
  • Direct Seed Funding Allocation: 100% of material subsidies, machine maintenance, and training stipends are auditable under cooperative accounting governance.

B. For Conscious Consumers & The General Public

  • Heirloom Quality Craftsmanship: Handcrafted utility goods engineered with heavy-duty denim offcuts, brass zippers, and reinforced double-stitching.
  • Direct Community Reinvestment: 100% of retail sales directly finance member wages, operational allowances, and community emergency healthcare funds.
  • Frictionless Accessible Ordering: Online catalog supporting GCash, BPI UP Campus bank transfer, and direct on-campus cash pickup at Pook Dagohoy.

C. For the PWD Community (Artisans & Members)

  • Adaptive Barrier-Free Workstations: Specially adapted sewing and cutting machinery accommodating wheelchair users, deaf-mute artisans, and low-vision sewers.
  • Dual Income Security: Guaranteed DTR Hourly Allowance (₱85/hr) plus Itemized Piece-Rate Earnings for every single unit completed.
  • Empowerment Through Tech: Multi-modal accessibility tools (high contrast, dyslexia font, audio chimes, voice guidance) allowing independent shift logging.
